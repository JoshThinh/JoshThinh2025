Experience

Software Engineering Intern, Brevvie

Summer 2026 to present

Trained the computer vision model that checks what customers put into Brevvie's propane-exchange lockers. It classifies each tank as steel, composite, or no tank, and it's now rolling out to hundreds of lockers across the US.

  • Now designing a cylinder validation step for unattended machines: the customer photographs the tank, the photo is checked by the model, and the exchange continues only if it passes. The feature is switched on per customer through a config flag.
  • Planned the pipeline around it: photos stored per order in S3, inference triggered through a queue, a human-review queue that feeds a weekly retraining cycle, and a three-attempt limit before an automatic refund.
  • Worked on the system's sign-in and logging.
